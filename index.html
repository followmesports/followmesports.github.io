01 / The camp

A camp day built around play.

Follow Me Sports Camps brings elementary-age kids onto a university campus for a full day of sport, coaching, and teamwork. The details of each camp are still being written.

Move

Try the sports.

Stations, games, and enough variety that a camper can find something they like.

Learn

Get coached.

College athletes teach skills in a way that stays encouraging.

Belong

Play on a team.

The day is shared. Campers practice showing up for each other.

02 / Mentors

College athletes in the huddle.

Mentors are college athletes. They coach, encourage, and spend the day with a small group of campers.

  • Coach They teach the sport in front of them, then the next one.
  • Encourage The point is a camper willing to try, not a highlight reel.
  • Stay close Groups stay small enough for a mentor to notice each camper.
