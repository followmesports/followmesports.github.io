Follow Me Sports Camps is a summer day camp on a university campus for kids entering kindergarten through 5th grade, led by college student-athletes.

Join the interest list
01 / Mentors

Mentors

College student-athletes, trained to care for your child, deliver a curriculum of athletic movement, sport-specific skill development, and age-appropriate personal development and leadership workshops.

02 / A typical day

A typical day

Drop-off is 8:30 to 9 a.m. Pickup is 3 to 3:30 p.m., or 5 to 5:30 p.m. with extended day. Campers bring a packed lunch. Afternoons are competitive games and student-athlete workshops.

03 / Ages

Ages

Camp is for kids entering kindergarten through 5th grade. Campers are in two groups: kindergarten through 2nd grade, and 3rd through 5th grade.

04 / Sessions

Sessions

Families choose the week-long sessions they want.

06 / For universities

For universities

Follow Me Sports Camps operates a premium summer day camp for kindergarten through 5th grade on a university campus. Follow Me pays a facility rental fee, staffs and insures the camp, and trains paid student-athlete mentors.

If this is a summer you want on your campus, email grant@followmesports.com and josh@followmesports.com.

07 / Experience

Terra Education

Follow Me Sports Camps is part of Terra Education.

Summer Springboard, Terra's pre-college program, runs on university campuses in the United States and abroad.

GLASS, the Girls Leadership Academy for Service and Sport, takes high school girls to Costa Rica for 10 days of sport, service, and leadership. The program is staffed by recent college athlete graduates who are trained and supported in delivering the program.

Beyond Sports has run sports programs for high school and college student-athletes since 2010. Student-Athletes Abroad, a Beyond Sports program, created international internships in which student-athletes earned academic credit through their home university.

Grant Leslie and Josh Erickson co-founded Beyond Sports.
