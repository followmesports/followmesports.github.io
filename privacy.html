Privacy policy

This notice covers the interest list on the Follow Me Sports Camps home page. To ask a question or request deletion, use the email address in the site footer.

What we ask for

  • Your name
  • Email address

We do not ask for a child's name, birthdate, school, street address, photo, or medical information. Please do not put those details in any field.

Why we ask

We use the email to write with news about sessions and registration. The name is so a reply can be addressed to a person.

Where it goes

This page does not store the form on its own. Until the interest list is connected, the form does not send your answers anywhere. It asks you to use the email address in the site footer instead. When the list is connected, a submission is added to a private Google Sheet kept by Follow Me Sports Camps, and a notice may be emailed to that same address. We do not sell this information.

How long we propose to keep it

Proposed rule, pending review: delete the row when you ask, and do not keep it longer than 24 months after the last message we send you about camps. Use the email address in the site footer and ask us to delete your interest-list row. A copy may remain in the notification mailbox until that mailbox is cleared on the same schedule. That mailbox practice still needs to be confirmed.

Who else handles it

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Google would store the Sheet, and deliver the notification email, only after the interest list is connected. We do not send the form fields to an analytics tool.
